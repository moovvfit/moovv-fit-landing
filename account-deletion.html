Account Deletion

Last updated: September 28, 2026

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Moovv account and all associated data at any time. We offer two ways to do this:

Option 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Moovv app
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm your decision

Your account will be scheduled for deletion immediately.

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to privacy@moovv.fit with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • Your registered phone number (if applicable)

We will process your request within 7 business days and send confirmation once complete.

Request Account Deletion via Email

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone)
  • Exercise history and progress data
  • Pain logs and health tracking data
  • Appointment history
  • All personal preferences and settings

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for a limited period as required by law (e.g., financial records for tax purposes) or to prevent fraud. This retained data is not used for any other purpose and is permanently deleted after the retention period.

Questions?

If you have questions about account deletion or data privacy, contact us at privacy@moovv.fit.
