Appointment booking

Patients book slots that fit your calendar — in-clinic visits or telehealth sessions, each on its own booking track. Reminders reduce no-shows without you lifting a finger.

High-quality exercise prescriptions

Prescribe from a physio-reviewed library of HD exercise videos with sets, reps, holds and progressions — in a couple of clicks, not a couple of printouts.

Export prescriptions as PDF

One click turns any plan into a clean, branded PDF — for patients who prefer paper, referring doctors, or insurance paperwork.

Plan notes for patients

Attach guidance, precautions and encouragement right where patients see their exercises — so your clinical voice travels home with them.

Pain & adherence tracking

Patients log pain and completion in the Moovv app after every session. You see who's improving, who's struggling and who's gone quiet — before the next visit.

AI insights

Moovv's AI reads pain trends and adherence patterns across your caseload and surfaces the patients who need your attention — with suggested next steps.

1-click messaging

Check in on a patient with a single tap using smart, context-aware messages — no typing the same "how's the knee feeling?" twenty times a day.

Add every physio in your practice and give your front desk their own receptionist login with its own permissions. Run several locations under one account and move physios between them as your rota changes.

Walk-in queue management

Reception adds walk-ins to the day's queue with a name, number and note. Physios see who's waiting and pick the next patient when they're free — no shouting across the clinic, no paper register.
