# Domain Context

## 🎯 Moovv Platform
AI-driven healthtech for digital MSK (musculoskeletal) and mobility care. Democratizing physical therapy through personalized, accessible solutions.

## 👥 Target Audiences

### Patients (Root Landing)
- People with MSK pain/mobility issues
- Seeking affordable PT alternatives
- Value: AI-guided exercises, progress tracking, accessibility

### Physiotherapists (for-physios/)
- Independent PTs and small practices
- Want to scale patient reach
- Value: Patient management, exercise prescription, remote monitoring

### Clinics (for-clinics/)
- Multi-practitioner clinics
- Need enterprise features
- Value: Team dashboard, telehealth integration, analytics

## 📄 Page Purpose

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `index.html` | Convert patients → app download |

| `for-clinics/index.html` | Convert clinics → enterprise inquiry |

## 💬 Messaging Tone
- **Warm, approachable** — not clinical
- **Empowering** — "take control of your health"
- **Simple** — avoid medical jargon
- **Trustworthy** — evidence-based, professional
