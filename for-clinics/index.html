Scale your network

Built to grow with your ambitions

Your first clinic is just the beginning. Moovv scales with you — from solo practice to multi-location chain, without changing platforms or migrating data.

  • Start with one clinic. Begin with a single location using the Physio Portal. All your data, patients, and workflows are ready to scale.
  • Add locations seamlessly. Open new branches in minutes. Staff can access their location immediately while you see everything from HQ.
  • Optimize with data. Use cross-location insights to identify what works. Replicate success, address issues before they spread.
  • Grow without surprises. No per-seat licenses, no hidden fees. Your cost scales with patients treated, not people employed.
